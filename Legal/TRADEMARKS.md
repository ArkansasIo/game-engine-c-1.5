## Naming Rules

1. Do not use a third-party game title as the project name.
2. Do not create source namespaces or asset IDs using a third-party trademark.
3. Use original game, engine, character, world, item, and faction names.
4. Keep third-party names in `References/` when they are needed for comparative research.
5. Run trademark, domain, app-store, and social-handle clearance before finalizing a commercial name.
